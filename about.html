About The Local AI Stack

A community-driven resource for running AI models on your own hardware

📖 What It Is

A community-driven resource for running AI models on your own hardware. Born from the Level1Techs forum, built for the broader local AI community.

🔄 How It Works

Community members submit their setups. We parse, organize, and display the data. The more submissions, the better the resource.

🤝 Connection to L1Techs

This project originated in the Machine Learning, LLMs & AI category on Level1Techs. The forum thread is where the discussion happens — this site is where the reference lives.

✍️ How to Contribute

Head to the forum thread and use the submission template in Post #2. Quick Submit takes 30 seconds.
