<div class="callout"><p><b>The 30-second version:</b> The matrix runs on community data. If you run a local LLM setup, share it — no account, no server, no guesswork. Fill in the form and we'll assemble a ready-to-post <code>[COMMUNITY-SUBMISSION]</code> block. You can <b>copy it for the forum thread</b> or <b>open a pre-filled GitHub issue</b>, whichever you prefer. Your name is always credited.</p></div>

## Why submit?

Every row in the <a href="../matrix.html">Matrix</a> is a real setup someone is actually running. Your data helps answer the site's core question — *what should I run, on what I have, for what I need?* — for the next person with your hardware. A 30-second submission is what turns a one-off report into a verified, citable data point.

## How it works

1. **Fill in the form.** The required fields are your VRAM tier, hardware, backend, model, quant, primary use case, and a few notes. Everything else is optional — but the more you share, the more useful your entry is.
2. **Pick your path.** Copy the block straight to the <a href="https://forum.level1techs.com/t/256443" target="_blank" rel="noopener">forum thread</a> (the original home of the data), or open a pre-filled GitHub issue.
3. **We integrate it.** Submissions are reviewed weekly and parsed into the dataset — your entry appears in the Matrix and the forum index, credited to you.

<div class="callout callout--tip"><p><b>Not sure about a field?</b> Leave the optional ones blank. A quick report with just your GPU, model, and "it works" is genuinely valuable — it corroborates other submissions and helps the matrix converge on the truth.</p></div>
