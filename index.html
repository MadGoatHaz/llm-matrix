🔄 How It Works

1. 📝 Submit

Head to the forum thread and use the Quick Submit template in Post #2. 30 seconds: tier, hardware, model, speed.

2. ⚙️ We Parse

The pipeline pulls the thread, parses every submission, and organizes it by VRAM tier, hardware vendor, backend, and model.

3. 📊 You Browse

Filter the matrix by your hardware. See exactly what the community runs, at what speed, in what context.
